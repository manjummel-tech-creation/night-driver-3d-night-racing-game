Night Driver

Fifty kilometres of night, from the snowy peaks of Hakone in the south to the farms around Hokuto in the north. Four expressways meet at stacked interchanges; mountain passes, lake roads and farm lanes link five cities. Weave through traffic for cash, keep your tank full, spend your money in the cities' tune shops and the far-off towns that sell the rare parts, and keep an eye on your mirrors: if the police see you, they come after you.
